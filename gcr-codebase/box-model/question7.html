Typography and Text Styling

The Importance of Good Typography

Good typography makes a webpage easier to read and gives it a clean and professional appearance.

A serif font can give text a traditional and formal look. Font size and font weight can also be changed to make important information stand out.

A sans-serif font looks simple and modern. Proper line-height and letter spacing make long paragraphs easier to read.

Using highlighted text, strong text, and emphasized text helps readers notice important information.
