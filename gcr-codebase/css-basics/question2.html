Welcome to Mathura

Mathura is a famous city in Uttar Pradesh and is known for its historical and cultural importance.

About the City

Mathura is known as the birthplace of Lord Krishna. The city has many temples, ghats and historical places. It attracts many visitors throughout the year.

Famous Places

Some popular places to visit in Mathura are Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi, Vishram Ghat and Dwarkadhish Temple. These places are important parts of the city's history and culture.

Famous Food

Mathura is also famous for its delicious sweets. Mathura peda is one of the most popular sweets enjoyed by visitors and local people.
